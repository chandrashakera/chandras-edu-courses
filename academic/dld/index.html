Frequently Asked Questions

How do I earn the certificate?

Pass the quiz in all 30 topics across the five units, then claim your verifiable certificate on this page. You need a free account so your quiz results are saved.

What is the pass mark?

Each topic ends with a 5-question quiz. You need 3 out of 5 to pass. You can retake any quiz at any time, and your best score is kept.

How can someone verify my certificate?

Every certificate has its own ID, a QR code and a link. Opening the link, or scanning the QR code, shows the certificate on its verification page on this site.
