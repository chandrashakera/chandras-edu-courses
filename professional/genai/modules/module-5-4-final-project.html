Final Project — Your AI Toolkit

⏱ 12 min read 📝 Assignment inside

This is the final sub-module. You have covered 19 sub-modules across 5 modules — from what Generative AI is and how Indian companies use it, through prompt engineering, to RAG, agents, fine-tuning, automation, certifications, profile building and freelancing.

The final project brings all of that together in one living document: your Personal AI Toolkit. It is not a certificate you print and forget. It is a practical reference you will use every week — in your job, in placement interviews and in freelance work.

What the AI Toolkit is

Your AI Toolkit is a single shareable document that collects the tools, prompts, workflows and plans you built during this course. It serves four concrete purposes:

  • Interview evidence — instead of saying "I know GenAI", you can show exactly how you have applied it to real tasks.
  • A daily work reference — your tested prompts and workflows are one search away whenever a familiar task comes up.
  • A freelance portfolio — a link you can send to a potential client to show what you can deliver.
  • A living record of your skills — updated every few months, it shows how your AI skills grow over time.

Each section maps to practice work you have already done in this course. The final project is assembly, not creation from scratch.

Keep it honest: include only work you actually did, and remove any client-confidential or personal data before you share it.

How to build and share your Toolkit

Format. Use a document tool you already open daily, such as Google Docs or Notion. A notes tool like Notion makes it easy to search, tag and share; Google Docs is fine if you already use it. Either is better than a PDF, because a PDF cannot be updated.

Sharing. Set the document to "Anyone with the link can view". The link becomes a living portfolio you can:

  • Add to your LinkedIn Featured section (Module 5.2)
  • Include in a freelance marketplace profile
  • Share with an interviewer: "Would you like to see how I have applied GenAI to real tasks?"
  • Send to a potential freelance client alongside your CV

Updating. Set a reminder every three months to review the Toolkit: re-test your top prompts, update your tool choices, add new workflows and refresh your 90-day plan. An out-of-date Toolkit is worse than none, because it shows old skills as current.

What comes next

  • Use it every week. The skills in this course grow with use. Add one prompt to your library every week.
  • Follow your 90-day plan. Complete one certification and one portfolio project you can explain in an interview.
  • Keep checking. Tools, prices and rules change. Verify facts, date what you save, and re-test regularly.
  • Go deeper where your role needs it. Developers can build a small RAG or agent project; analysts can automate a real report; managers can lead a team AI policy or training.

Your final project

This is the course capstone. Block 2–3 hours — ideally a weekend session — and build it from start to finish. You build it in the form below, so your work is saved and you can download it.

  1. Fill in the seven sections of the form below with the practice work you kept in your AI Learning Journal. Each section shows its minimum.
  2. Click Save draft whenever you like — your draft is also saved automatically every 30 seconds while you type, and it is here when you come back.
  3. When all seven sections show ✓, tick the confirmation box and click Submit Final Project. Submitting completes this sub-module and unlocks your certificate once the other 19 are passed.
  4. Download your toolkit as a Markdown file or a PDF and keep it.
  5. Remove any personal or client-confidential information before you share it.
  6. Paste it into a Google Doc or Notion page titled "My Personal AI Toolkit — [Your Name] — [Month Year]" and generate a view-only sharing link.
  7. Add the link to your LinkedIn Featured section, so recruiters who view your profile can see it.
  8. Optional: share your Toolkit in a LinkedIn post explaining what you learned and what you built, with the hashtags #GenAIZeroToJobReady and #ChandrasEDU. Sharing publicly helps you commit to your 90-day plan.

Your Toolkit is now your main career asset for AI-related work. Update it every few months, add to your prompt library every week, and revisit your research before every interview. The course ends here — the Toolkit does not.
