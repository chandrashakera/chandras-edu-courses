ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini

⏱ 12 min read 📝 Assignment inside

In Module 1.1 you learned that ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are all built on Large Language Models that predict the next word. So if they work the same way under the hood, why does it matter which one you use?

It matters because each tool is built and packaged differently. They differ in what they connect to, how they handle long documents, what their free plans allow, and how they are priced. Picking the wrong tool for a task does not just give you weaker output. It can cost you time, and your credibility, when the output is wrong.

By the end of this sub-module you will know which tool to open for which task, and why.

Last checked: October 2026. AI tools change every few months: new models, new features, new limits and new prices. This page compares the tools at the level of what they are built for, not exact numbers. Before you pay for anything, check the official pages: ChatGPT plans, Claude plans and Gemini.

The three tools at a glance

Before going deep, here is the honest one-line summary for each:

  • ChatGPT (OpenAI) — the all-rounder: text, images, files, voice and web search in one app, with the largest ecosystem of add-ons and tutorials.
  • Claude (Anthropic) — the careful reader and coder: strong at long documents, structured writing and complex programming work.
  • Gemini (Google) — the connected researcher: built around Google Search and Google's own apps such as Gmail, Docs and Sheets.

Now let us go deeper on each one.

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

Strengths:

  • Most versatile: handles text, images, files, data analysis and voice in one place
  • Largest ecosystem: custom GPTs, app integrations and the most third-party tutorials
  • Built-in web search for current information
  • Fast and convenient for everyday tasks: emails, summaries, drafts and quick explanations

Watch out for:

  • Free-plan usage limits, which change from time to time
  • Confident tone even when it is wrong, so verify facts, figures and citations
  • For very long documents or large codebases, results can vary; test it on your own material before relying on it

Claude (Anthropic)

Strengths:

  • Strong at complex coding tasks and understanding large amounts of code
  • Handles long documents well: reports, research papers, contracts and lengthy code files
  • Writes in a natural, structured tone that usually needs less editing
  • Good at careful, multi-step reasoning
  • Claude Code is a dedicated agentic coding tool for developers

Watch out for:

  • Free-plan message limits can run out quickly during heavy use
  • A smaller add-on ecosystem than ChatGPT
  • Like every model, it can still be wrong, so check anything important

Gemini (Google)

Strengths:

  • Built around Google Search, which makes it handy for current events and quick research
  • Works inside Google Workspace apps such as Gmail, Docs and Sheets
  • Handles long inputs such as large documents
  • A generous free plan for everyday use

Watch out for:

  • Writing style can be less polished than Claude for long-form text
  • Answers can vary between similar questions, so re-check important ones
  • Search-backed answers still need you to open and read the sources

A practical setup for Indian students

You do not need to pay for any of these tools to get significant value. Here is the free setup that covers most use cases:

  • ChatGPT (free) — your default for everyday questions, drafts and quick explanations.
  • Claude (free) — for long documents, careful writing and code you need to understand properly.
  • Gemini (free) — for current information and anything inside Google apps.

All three have free plans. Open accounts on all three today — the assignment will ask you to use them. If you later want a paid plan, compare current prices on each official pricing page and check whether they accept UPI or Indian cards. India pricing and payment options change often, which is why this page does not list prices.

One important warning about all three

All three can be confidently wrong. They can invent facts, statistics, quotes and even references that look real. The more polished the answer looks, the easier it is to trust it without checking. Before you submit, share or act on any AI output, verify every fact, number and citation against an original source. You will learn why this happens, and how to handle it, in Module 1.4.

The three things to remember

  • ChatGPT for everyday tasks — the most versatile all-rounder, best for quick work and placement prep.
  • Claude for code and long documents — best for serious coding tasks and careful reading of long material.
  • Gemini for research and Google tools — built around Google Search and works inside Gmail, Docs and Sheets.
