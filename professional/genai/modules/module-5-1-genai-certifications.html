Certifications Worth Getting

⏱ 12 min read 📝 Assignment inside

Welcome to Module 5 — the final module. You have completed the technical foundations of this course. This module is about turning that knowledge into career outcomes: the right certifications, a strong profile, freelancing opportunities, and a final project that pulls everything together.

This sub-module answers a question almost every engineering student asks: which AI certifications are actually worth the time and money? The honest answer depends on your current role, your target role, and what you can show alongside the certificate.

Last checked: October 2026. Course names, exam codes and fees change often. Every option below links to its official page — check the current details there before you enrol or pay.

What Indian employers actually look for

Before listing certifications, the honest context: a certificate shows you studied something; a project shows you can do something. Recruiters skim profiles quickly, so a recognised name helps you get noticed — but in interviews, what you built and how you explain it matters far more.

Think of AI credentials in three tiers:

  1. Proctored vendor certifications — exams from cloud and technology companies such as Google Cloud and Microsoft. Harder to earn, so they carry the most weight, especially for technical roles.
  2. Platform certificates from known providers — course certificates on platforms like Coursera from Google, IBM or DeepLearning.AI. Good evidence of structured learning, especially for freshers.
  3. Short-course badges — free short courses and completion badges. Useful for learning, but light as a hiring signal on their own.

Above all three sits your portfolio: one real project on GitHub, a case study or a working automation that you can explain in an interview.

The certifications worth getting — ranked for Indian students

Free first — build the foundation

  • Generative AI for Everyone (DeepLearning.AI, on Coursera) — Andrew Ng's non-technical introduction to what GenAI can and cannot do. Free to start; a paid certificate is optional.
  • ChatGPT Prompt Engineering for Developers (DeepLearning.AI with OpenAI) — a free short course with hands-on notebooks. Needs basic Python. It is a learning resource, not a certification.
  • Microsoft Learn — free modules on AI fundamentals and Copilot, which also prepare you for Microsoft's certification exams.
  • FutureSkills Prime — free foundation courses in AI and other emerging technologies (see the India-specific section below).

India-specific option — NASSCOM FutureSkills Prime

FutureSkills Prime is a joint skilling initiative of NASSCOM and the Ministry of Electronics and IT (MeitY). It offers many free courses in AI, data, cloud and cybersecurity, plus paid industry-aligned courses. Under the government's FutureSkills Prime Incentive Program, eligible learners can get part of the fee of eligible paid courses reimbursed after completing the course and its assessment. Check eligibility and current incentive amounts on the official scheme page before you enrol.

It is especially useful if you are targeting domestic IT hiring or want a government-recognised record of your skills.

Recommended paths by role

Developers (or students aiming for developer roles)

  • Month 1: Generative AI for Everyone (free) for the big picture
  • Month 2: ChatGPT Prompt Engineering for Developers (free short course)
  • Month 3–4: A portfolio project on GitHub — for example, a small RAG app from Module 4.1
  • Later: Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-901); Google Cloud Professional ML Engineer only after real ML project experience
  • Skip: non-technical business AI certificates

Data and business analysts

  • Month 1: Google AI Essentials or Generative AI for Everyone
  • Month 2: Microsoft Learn modules on Copilot and AI fundamentals (free)
  • Month 3: A portfolio project — an AI-assisted analysis or a simple automation workflow
  • Optional: Google Cloud Generative AI Leader if you work closely with business stakeholders
  • Skip: coding-heavy developer certifications

Final-year students and freshers (any branch)

  • Week 1–2: Generative AI for Everyone (free)
  • Week 3–4: The free course that matches your target role from the lists above
  • Month 2: One portfolio project (an automation or a prompt library)
  • Month 3: Pay for one recognised certificate — or apply for financial aid (below)
  • Add: a FutureSkills Prime course if you are targeting domestic IT hiring

Working professionals, team leads and managers

  • Month 1: Generative AI for Everyone or Google AI Essentials
  • Month 2: Microsoft Learn Copilot modules (free)
  • Month 3: Google Cloud Generative AI Leader
  • Month 4: Your company's internal AI certification, if available
  • Skip: coding-heavy developer or cloud architecture certifications

The financial aid route — getting paid certificates free

Many Coursera courses offer financial aid that covers the course fee, including the certificate. Not every course offers it, so check the course page.

Process: open the course on Coursera → look for the "Financial aid available" link near the enrol button → answer the questions about your background, finances and goals → submit. Applications are made per course, so a specialization with several courses needs several applications. Coursera reviews each application, which typically takes up to about two weeks; you can usually start learning while you wait. If you are approved, finish the course within the time Coursera gives you.

What to write: be specific and honest. For example: "I am a final-year BTech student in [city]. This certificate will help me in campus placements for [role]. I cannot currently afford the fee because [reason]." Never exaggerate — applications are reviewed by people.

An approved course gives you the same certificate as a paid learner.

The three things to remember

  • One recognised certificate plus one project beats a pile of badges — choose a certificate from a known provider that fits your target role, and pair it with a portfolio project you can explain. The project is what convinces an interviewer.
  • Start free, pay only for the signal — learn with free courses first; pay (or apply for financial aid) only for the one or two certificates that matter for your role.
  • Check before you pay — exam codes, fees and course names change. Always confirm the current details on the official page.

Practice (optional)

These tasks turn certification advice into a plan you can actually follow. It should take about 30 minutes.

  • Pick your path: choose the role path above that matches your target job. Write down the exact courses or certifications for the next three months.
  • Check the details: open the official page for each paid option on your plan and note the current fee, format and any prerequisites, with today's date.
  • Plan your project: write two sentences describing the portfolio project you will build alongside the certificate.
  • Financial aid (if needed): draft your Coursera financial aid answers for one course, using the guidance above.
  • Save it in your AI Learning Journal under "Module 5.1 — Certification Plan."
