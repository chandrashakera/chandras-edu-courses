AI Tools for Everyday Work

A free, practical course to use AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude for real college work: circulars, Excel, lesson plans, lab manuals and more. For teaching, office and lab staff. No coding needed. Certificate on completion.

📦 6 Parts 📄 13 Modules 🏆 Certificate 🆓 Free
Who is this course for?
  • Teaching staff: save time on lesson plans, question papers, feedback and reports.
  • Office staff: write circulars, emails, minutes and reports faster, and get help with Excel.
  • Lab assistants and system programmers: prepare lab manuals, safety rules, stock registers and simple scripts faster.

You do not need any technical knowledge. If you can use email and a web browser, you can do this course.

How each module works

Each module has the same five parts:

  1. Learn: a short, simple explanation.
  2. Copy and use: ready-made prompts. You fill in the blanks.
  3. See the difference: a weak request compared with a good one.
  4. Try it: a 10–20 minute task in a free AI tool.
  5. Quiz: 5 questions. You need 70% to pass. You can try again.

How to get the certificate

  1. Pass the quiz in every module of your path (70% or more).
  2. Complete the Final Project: My AI Work Kit. You pick one real job you do often and build a small kit for it: 5 prompts you have tried, one finished document made with AI, and 3 short reflection answers. It takes about one hour. At the end, you can print or save your kit as a PDF and keep using it at work. You start preparing for it in Module 4.

Each certificate has a unique ID. Anyone can check it online.

About the examples

All examples use a made-up college called Tarangini Institute of Technology. The names and details are invented. Any match with a real college is a coincidence. You will learn to use every idea with your own college's formats.

About the course design

This course is guided by the UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers (2024). The framework lists five areas: human-centred mindset, ethics of AI, AI foundations and applications, AI pedagogy, and AI for professional learning. This course covers the practical, everyday side of it, and also includes office and lab staff. This course is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by UNESCO. Read the framework ↗

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this course really free?

Yes, this course is free.

Do I need coding experience?

No. If you can use email and a web browser, you can do this course.

Which AI tools will I use?

Only free versions. Examples mention ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity and NotebookLM. The same ideas work in any similar tool. Tools change often, so Module 3 is updated regularly.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes — pass the quizzes for your path and complete the Final Project (My AI Work Kit) to unlock your certificate. Your certificate unlocks once all modules in your path are released.
